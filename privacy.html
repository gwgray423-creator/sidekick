Privacy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

The short version: no account, no login, no cookies, no ads, and nothing about you is ever sold. There is no profile of you to leak, because none is built.

What Side Kick collects

Almost nothing. Here is the complete list.

  • Anonymous visit counts. The site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It records aggregate numbers — how many people visited, and roughly where they came from (a referring site, or none). It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and does not track you across other websites.
  • Nothing you type. The question and options you enter are processed in your own browser. They are never sent to us and never stored.

What Side Kick does not collect

  • No name, no account, no password.
  • No advertising trackers, and no data sold or shared with data brokers.
  • No email list is running at the moment. If one is added later, this page will say exactly who stores your address before you can sign up.

Payments

Support payments are handled entirely by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and are never seen by, or stored on, Side Kick. Stripe's own privacy policy governs that data. Side Kick only learns that a payment succeeded — never a card number.

Shared links

When you share a decision, the question is passed in the link itself (the part after the ?). That means anyone you send the link to can read the question — so don't put anything private in it.

Analytics and your choices

Because GoatCounter sets no cookies, there is no cookie banner to click and nothing to opt out of. If you use an ad or script blocker, you are simply not counted, which is fine by us.

Children

Side Kick is safe for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Material changes will be described in plain language.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/gwgray423-creator/sidekick.
